## How extraction works

Each video is read **once** by `claude-sonnet-4-6` vision over 8–12 frames spread across the clip. Hook titles are separated from speech captions by **behaviour across frames** (a title is identical in every frame; captions change constantly), never by screen position.

A **second, offset-sampled read** runs only when pass 1's evidence is weak (`uncertain`, ≤2 frames coverage, captions present with patchy coverage) or when acting alone would discard a row (`multi_title_claim`, `no_title_claim`). Conflicts are quarantined for a human, never guessed.

Descriptions reuse `lib/providers/anthropic/vision.ts` **unchanged**, at exactly 8 frames with no options — reproducing the app's runtime call byte-for-byte, including its 16-second coverage limit. This is deliberate: retrieval compares corpus descriptions against the description the app generates for an uploaded video, and embedding similarity only holds if both sides come from the same prompt, model and sampling. **22% of videos are longer than 16s**, so those are described from their opening only.

## CRITICAL FINDING: the views column was corrupt

Full write-up: **`docs/findings/2026-08-02-view-metric-inconsistency.md`**

`scripts/scrape-instagram.ts` preferred `videoViewCount` over `videoPlayCount`. Instagram returns **both**, and they are different metrics disagreeing by **2.6×–130×** on the same video. ~88% of stored view values were 2–3× too low, with nothing marking which rows were affected.

**Fixed** (`926c72e`) and **repaired** (`f10f412` — `scripts/refresh-metrics.ts --apply`):
- 1,828 of 1,837 rows re-pulled from Apify; baselines, outlier multipliers, `viewsPerDay` and rank labels recomputed
- All 14 manifests backed up to `manifest.json.bak-<timestamp>` before writing
- Verified against an independent source: 562,527 vs 562,525 on a known row
- All 175 titles, 175 descriptions and 8 human overrides preserved

**This invalidated the metric analysis done earlier in the session** (engagement rate vs creator-relative view outlier, Spearman 0.05, the recommendation to use view-outlier percentile). **Do not act on that analysis.** It must be redone on corrected data.

Baselines moved unevenly by creator: `rowanrow` 0.97×, `bielvalldo` 1.05× (already correct) up to `julesfrankenn` 4.01×, `budrys.jr` 3.60× (badly affected).

### Selection impact (reported, not applied)

## Why retrieval was broken (and what fixes it)

`orchestrator.ts` embeds `scene + visualHook` and matches it against `corpus_titles.embedding`, which is the embedding of the **title**. Comparing a scene description to a hook title in embedding space is close to noise — retrieval was only weakly better than random. The `visual_description` column exists to enable description↔description matching, which is the comparison the pipeline was always meant to make.
