## Before scraping these

Three things from the existing corpus that bear directly on this list:

1. **Roughly 45% of scraped videos yield nothing usable.** On the 2026-08-07
   backfill, 137 videos produced 76 corpus rows: 23% had no burned-in title at
   all and 20% were quarantined as needs_review. Budget on usable rows, not on
   videos fetched.

2. **Every creator added shifts the ground truth.** `performance_score` is a
   percentile rank over the whole corpus and median share rate varies **10.4x
   between creators**, with creator identity alone explaining ~11.4% of its
   variance. A large intake of new creators changes what every existing score
   means. Resolving the within-creator percentile question first would make this
   intake much cleaner to interpret.

3. **Budget is the binding constraint, not the list length.** At the measured
   rates — 5 SocialCrawl credits per video, ~$0.0525 OCR, ~$0.0274 description —
   295 remaining credits cover about **59 videos total**, which is roughly one
   creator's worth of reels, not 69 creators'.
